# حكاية | HIKAYA

## Story spark identity

The original vector symbol is a compact open book with two broad, rising pages. Its right page contains a gold sail-shaped inset, a quiet reference to Omani seafaring. A coral four-point spark rises above the left page: the moment a child’s idea becomes a story. The page gap remains open and the silhouettes stay broad enough to work at favicon size.

The bilingual lockup pairs a bold Arabic **حكاية** wordmark with a carefully spaced **HIKAYA** signature. A small coral curve adds warmth without changing the Arabic letterforms. It has a transparent background and uses no raster lettering, gradients or fine decorative details.

- Emerald ink: `#164D42`
- Coral spark: `#E68068`
- Muted sail gold: `#C6A26C`
- Paper accent: `#FFF9EB`
- English signature ink: `#796746`

`dist/assets/hikaya-symbol.svg` is the scalable symbol for the website header, favicon and book covers. Its 96 × 96 viewBox leaves protective breathing room. Use at 32 pixels or larger when the inner sail and page strokes matter; the main silhouette also works smaller.

`dist/assets/hikaya-logo.svg` is the 520 × 170 transparent Arabic/English lockup. Its Arabic wordmark uses Noto Sans Arabic, with Tahoma/Arial fallbacks. Keep the website wordmark as accessible live text. For fixed raster sharing, render the PNG in the browser with the project’s locally bundled Noto Sans Arabic font loaded; do not rasterize Arabic via an engine that lacks shaping.

These SVG assets are original code-native artwork. Previous designs remain recoverable in source history. PNG rendering is maintained separately from the vector assets.
